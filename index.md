## My Favorite CPSC 120L Labs
**Lab 7**
This is my favorite lab! I enjoyed working with if statements to solve real world problems. It gave me a sense of how programs work in society. Having some experience with Python, I was curious to work with if statements in C++, since the syntax is different. Despite facing some challenges, my team and I were able to successfully complete this lab. 

 

**Lab 1** 
This lab, despite being the easiest out of the 12, was very exciting! I remember going into the lab feeling anxious, as I had never heard of, or worked using C++. Looking back, I think this was a great hands-on intro to C++. I also remember feeling excited when the program successfully ran! 

 

**Lab 4, Part 1** 
The first part of Lab 4 is one of my favorites! I enjoyed converting measurements in the metric system into units in the US customary system. Being more familiar with cout and cin, it was very satisfying to work with. It was also very satisfying to see how our program passed the test cases!
